FollowUp Buddy by Sakred Studio · Effective 2026-10-24

Short version: everything you enter stays on your phone. FollowUp Buddy has no server, no account, no analytics, and never sends a message on your behalf.

छोटा जवाब: आप जो भी लिखते हैं वह आपके फ़ोन पर ही रहता है। FollowUp Buddy का कोई सर्वर नहीं है, कोई अकाउंट नहीं, कोई ट्रैकिंग नहीं, और यह आपकी तरफ़ से कभी कोई मैसेज नहीं भेजता।

What the app stores

  • The leads you add: name, WhatsApp number or Instagram handle, what they asked for, tags, priority, status, follow-up dates and the history notes you write.
  • Your own settings: language, theme, default follow-up delay, and your name / handle / business type if you choose to enter them.

All of this is saved in your browser's local storage on your device (key fub.v1). It is not uploaded anywhere. If you uninstall the app or clear site data, it is gone, unless you used Settings → Backup & export to save a JSON file. Settings → Delete all data wipes it instantly.

What leaves your phone

  • WhatsApp follow-ups. When you tap WhatsApp on a lead, the app opens wa.me with a pre-written message. Nothing is sent until you press Send inside WhatsApp. The message includes the lead's first name, what they asked for, and a short "sent with FollowUp Buddy" line with the app link.
  • Instagram follow-ups. The app copies the same message to your clipboard and opens the lead's Instagram profile. You paste and send it yourself.
  • Share. The Share button hands a text summary of one lead to your phone's share sheet. Where you send it is up to you.
  • Backups. Export creates a JSON (or, on Pro, CSV) file on your device. Where you keep or share that file is up to you.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no cookies, no third-party fonts or scripts at runtime. The app works fully offline after the first load.

Your customers' data

The names and numbers you save belong to your customers. They stay on your phone only; the app never reads your contact list and never uploads anything. Please delete a lead if a customer asks you to.

Children

The app is a general-purpose tool for adults running a small business. It does not knowingly collect data from children.
