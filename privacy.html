FollowUp Buddy by Sakred Studio · Effective 2026-10-25 (v3)

Short version: everything you enter stays on your phone. FollowUp Buddy has no account, no analytics, and never sends a message on your behalf. The only optional server feature is the Pro encrypted cloud backup, which is off until you switch it on and can only ever see scrambled data.

छोटा जवाब: आप जो भी लिखते हैं वह आपके फ़ोन पर ही रहता है। FollowUp Buddy का कोई अकाउंट नहीं, कोई ट्रैकिंग नहीं, और यह आपकी तरफ़ से कभी कोई मैसेज नहीं भेजता। सिर्फ़ Pro का एन्क्रिप्टेड क्लाउड बैकअप एक वैकल्पिक सर्वर फ़ीचर है — यह तब तक बंद रहता है जब तक आप चालू न करें, और सर्वर को सिर्फ़ गड़बड़ किया हुआ डेटा दिखता है।

What the app stores

  • The leads you add: name, WhatsApp number or Instagram handle, what they asked for, tags, priority, status and pipeline stage, follow-up dates and the history notes you write (each note can carry the name you gave this phone).
  • Your own settings: language, theme, default follow-up delay, reminder hour and quiet hours, message templates, this phone's name, and your name / handle / business type / WhatsApp number if you choose to enter them.
  • Catalogue photos (v3): product photos you pick are resized on your phone to 800 px and stored in your browser's IndexedDB on this device. They leave the phone only inside a product card you share yourself.

All of this is saved in your browser's local storage on your device (key fub.v1) and IndexedDB (fub-photos). It is not uploaded anywhere unless you turn on cloud backup (below). If you uninstall the app or clear site data, it is gone, unless you used Settings → Backup & export to save a JSON file. Settings → Delete all data wipes it instantly, photos included.

What leaves your phone

  • WhatsApp follow-ups. When you tap WhatsApp on a lead, the app opens wa.me with a pre-written message (the default one or the template you attached to the lead's tag). Nothing is sent until you press Send inside WhatsApp. The message includes the lead's first name, what they asked for, and a short "sent with FollowUp Buddy" line with the app link.
  • Instagram follow-ups. The app copies the same message to your clipboard and opens the lead's Instagram profile. You paste and send it yourself.
  • Share. The Share button hands a text summary of one lead to your phone's share sheet. Send product card hands a product image + message to the share sheet (or saves the image to your downloads). Where you send it is up to you.
  • Share into the app. If you share a contact, profile link or text into FollowUp Buddy from another app, the app only reads what you shared to prefill a new lead. It never reads your contacts or chats.
  • "Send me today's list". Opens a WhatsApp chat with your own number and today's list pre-typed. You press Send.
  • Reminders. The daily "N leads today" notification is built on your phone (by the app or its service worker). No push server is involved.
  • Backups. Export creates a JSON (or, on Pro, CSV) file on your device. Where you keep or share that file is up to you.

Cloud backup & team sharing (Pro, optional)

  • Opt-in. Nothing is uploaded until you create a vault in Settings → Cloud backup. In builds where the backup server is not switched on, the screen says "Not available yet".
  • Encrypted on your phone. Your backup is encrypted on the device with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2, 200 000 rounds, SHA-256 → AES-GCM-256). The passphrase never leaves the phone. The server stores only scrambled data (salt, iv, data), a timestamp and a random vault code; it cannot read your leads, and neither can we. If you forget the passphrase the backup cannot be recovered.
  • Identity. The random 32-character vault code is the only identifier. No account, no email, no phone number is sent.
  • Team of two. Sharing with an assistant means giving them the same vault code and passphrase; both phones then read and write the same encrypted vault. Anyone holding both can read and edit the list, so share the passphrase in person.
  • Deleting. Delete cloud copy removes the vault from the server. Forget vault on this phone removes the code and key from this phone only.
  • Server. A Cloudflare Worker + D1 database operated by Sakred Studio; data cap 2 MB per vault; request rate limits per IP; no logs of vault contents.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no cookies, no third-party fonts or scripts at runtime. The app works fully offline after the first load.

Your customers' data

The names and numbers you save belong to your customers. They stay on your phone only; the app never reads your contact list and never uploads anything. Please delete a lead if a customer asks you to.

Children

The app is a general-purpose tool for adults running a small business. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

फ़ोन से बाहर क्या जाता है: सिर्फ़ वह WhatsApp या Instagram मैसेज या प्रोडक्ट कार्ड जो आप खुद भेजते हैं। ऐप मैसेज तैयार करके चैट खोलता है; Send आप दबाते हैं।
