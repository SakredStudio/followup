Privacy Policy — FollowUp Buddy

FollowUp Buddy is an offline-first personal lead follow-up organizer built by Sakred Studio.

1. 100% Local-First Data Storage

All leads, contact handles, notes, tags, and follow-up schedules are stored locally on your device (`localStorage` key `fub.v1`). We do not upload your customer list or notes to external servers.

2. WhatsApp & Instagram Links

When you tap WhatsApp or Instagram on a lead, FollowUp Buddy opens the official `wa.me` or `instagram.com` link in your browser or app so you can send the message yourself. FollowUp Buddy never sends automated messages on your behalf.

3. Backups & Exports

You can export your data to JSON or CSV at any time from Settings, or clear all data from your browser storage.
